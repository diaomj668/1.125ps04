## Actual production method

The frozen scripts were narrated using the configured model provider's `tts-1` endpoint, voice `alloy`. Seven original text-card scenes per video were rendered locally using FFmpeg. Each complete source video was uploaded to Google Vids. English styled captions were added in Vids, and the final draft MP4 was exported to Drive through the Vids interface. This was an import, captioning, and export workflow; Google Vids did not generate the narration or original visuals. AI-generated narration is disclosed in the videos.

The editor timelines confirmed complete durations of about 3:51, 4:07, and 4:07. The second exported MP4 was observed playing in Drive with a 4:07 duration. The other Drive previews were initially still processing. A local copy of a Google Vids export was not obtained; `media/*/source-video.mp4` files are retained source assets, not Vids exports.
