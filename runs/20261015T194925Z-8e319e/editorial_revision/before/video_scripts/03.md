**Narration:** For a performance question, the verification setup itself matters. Simon Willison reports that 95.1 percent of recorded stacks in his web-edit profiling example came from AnyIO’s worker thread. A main-thread-only profile would have missed them. Sampling periodically captures snapshots of execution. Before comparing an assistant’s proposed change, reproduce a relevant workload and include the threads doing the work. Sample frequencies alone do not establish elapsed-time savings. Python version compatibility and permission requirements also need checking.
